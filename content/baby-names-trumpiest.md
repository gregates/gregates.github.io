One of my favorite public datasets is the baby name data published every year by the United States
Social Security Administration (SSA). I like to do little analyses of this data in [Row
Zero](https://rowzero.com) from time to time, such as my earlier exploration of the popularity of
baby names [by final letter](/baby-names-rise-of-n).

My latest project is a report on which baby names had the most partisan political valence in 2024
&mdash; the Trumpiest (and least Trumpy) baby names. The general idea is to identify which names are
more popular in red states vs. blue states. You can get a copy of the report, along with
all the SSA data through 2024 and the calculations I did, by [copying my
workbook](https://rowzero.com/workbook/197192F7CFA3241FB175B901/3?copy). Or if you just want the
summary report, here it is:
